# poplar.agency

A neighborhood marketing agency for independent restaurants, bars, cafes, retailers, and other small businesses.

## Core idea

Poplar helps local businesses fill slow periods and turn first-time visitors into regulars through coordinated campaigns, events, social discovery, physical collectibles, digital membership, and measurable follow-up.

The agency is positioned as a managed local customer-growth and loyalty network—not a generic social-media service and not a discount marketplace.

## Strategy

The current strategy and implementation document is in [STRATEGY.md](./STRATEGY.md). It covers:

- Business proposition and initial Chicago neighborhood wedge
- Merchant and customer marketing strategy
- Neighborhood Passport campaign design
- Physical passport, stamps, QR, and NFC experiences
- Software, measurement, and optional Atmosphere/AT Protocol layers
- Hardware and collectible artifacts
- 90-day validation workstream
- Pilot design, risks, and product roadmap

## Status

This repository currently contains the working strategy document. Product implementation should begin only after merchant interviews validate the initial neighborhood, slow-period problem, campaign economics, and willingness to pay.
